# Quality bar — proposed production target

Applies to school G1 first, then every chapter. This proposal does not certify
the baseline; acceptance cues belong in REVIEW_QUEUE.

**Art direction.** A Vietnamese school marked by water, repair and everyday use:
worn plaster, uneven paint, simple wood desks, paper and oxidized broadcast
equipment. Human-scale architecture, credible material joins, readable clues.
References: user V3 and original modelling sources. Record provenance for any
external reference. Decorative repetition must not obscure evidence.

**Lighting/materials.** Restrained cool night palette, local warm practicals.
Darkness retains navigable edges and clue readability; no unexplained saturated
wash, clipped surfaces or invisible doorway. Roughness conveys age; normals
must not turn plaster into gravel. Collision matches visible dimensions.

**Animation/interaction.** Controllable camera; prompts point to the investigated
object and stop out of reach. Distinct feedback for collection, playback,
listening and supernatural reactions. Sensitivity and reduced-motion options.

**UI/Vietnamese.** Responsive safe areas at 1280x720 and 1920x1080. Main text
at least 18 px at 720p, full diacritics, wrapped lines, readable contrast.
No journal overlap; keyboard navigation and actual bindings visible. Captions
and transcripts for critical evidence; speaker identity follows story knowledge.

**Audio.** Masked and clarified recordings convey distinct information, with
loudness/spectrum reports and independent voice/effects controls. Investigation
works with captions. User must listen to specified beats; automated reports
cannot establish quality or fear. Synthesis is not final Vietnamese dialogue.

**Performance.** Provisional minimum: discovered Core Ultra 7 155H, 15.5 GB RAM,
integrated Intel Arc; 1280x720 low, sustained 30 fps (33.3 ms frame budget).
Validate a standalone build, median/p95 frame time and peak memory. Target
awaits measurement. Bound shadows/textures; editor fps is insufficient.

**Accessibility/acceptance.** Remappable controls, toggle/hold listening,
subtitle scale, reduced motion and no evidence encoded by colour alone. A new
player can enter, find evidence, compare recordings, finish a sentence and
understand attention consequences without developer commands. Broken spawn,
unreachable rooms, unreadable text or bypassed gates fail the slice.
