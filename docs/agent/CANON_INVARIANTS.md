## 1. Fixed facts

- The sluice gate B opens at 02:17:17 on the night of 20-21 September 2002, after a 27-minute delay (01:50 to 02:17), while 65 people in basement B are still able to answer. (V3 section 3)
- Six things do not change with the player's choices: the gate opens at 02:17:17; 65 people answered the final check; there was a 27-minute delay; Nhi really died in the basement; Khải begged his father at 02:10; ông Thạch knew there were people in the basement from 23:40. (V3 section 3, "Sự thật cố định")
- Choices only change how the player discovers these facts, who is harmed, and what happens after the facts become known. (V3 section 3)
- 67 people entered the basement, 2 left (bà Mai, Lệ), so 65 real victims and 65 answering voices. (V3 section 3, headcount table derivations)
- 64 names still valid in the basement ledger, minus Lệ (survivor), gives 63 official names, which are the 63 names carved on the memorial wall. (V3 section 3)
- The two sets of 63 are two different sets of people: cô Vân's group includes bà Mai and Lệ, the wall includes cô Vân and ông Đạt. The ledger's 64 is not cô Vân's number; no NPC may confuse them in dialogue. (V3 section 3, deliberate trap)
- The game has no combat system and no sanity blur bar; failure is concrete loss and is kept. (V3 section 2, V3 section 8.5)
- 12 nights, from night 9 to 10 September to night 20 to 21 September, exactly 24 years after the flood. (V3 section 2)
- Structure: Prologue, 7 chapters, Epilogue. (V3 section 2)
- The system is Blueprint-first; the story canon is read-only. (AGENTS.md, kl-project-context) UNSURE: V3 itself does not state the engine.

## 3. Recordings and the "listen to the whole sentence" mechanic

- T1 is the operations tape from the control room: the original is complete; the edited version (Sương, 05:00) deleted the 01:50 and 02:10 segments, leaving only the fragment "Cha biết." In Hồi 3 Hà has an illegal intermediate copy: 02:16:52 intact, the other two gone. (V3 section 3)
- T2 is bà Sương's switchboard tape (speaker and phone): the 23:40 order, Nhi's 01:58 call, and the 43-second segment. The nightly broadcast is not erased but masked: 65 answers mixed under noise. (V3 section 3)
- T3 is the automatic recorder on the internal phone line in basement B, with a call log. It is the independent source: nobody in Căn phòng 02:00 edited it because nobody knew it existed until anh Tư điện found the wiring diagram. (V3 section 3)
- The player never has "T2" in general; each of the three versions unlocks a different level of discovery: masked nightly broadcast (from night 1), archived backup copy (Hồi 3-5, at most 63 voices; the two smallest voices stay below threshold), unprocessed original (Hồi 6, sealed vault, all 65 voices). (V3 section 3)
- "Nghe cho hết câu" steps: find the cut, collect candidates from other sources (a missing part needs at least two sources, one independent), Thử nghe (rates giọng/nhịp/tiếng phòng as khớp/lệch/không rõ, never right or wrong), Niêm phong (costs time and noise, raising Sự chú ý), result (correct opens the real Khoảng Lặng; wrong opens a false one). (V3 section 8.2)
- Cut sentences of the same event are sealed as a set (usually three); if only one or two are right the game says "đúng một phần" without saying which. (V3 section 8.2)
- T1 is a related source, T3 is the independent one. (V3 section 3, V3 section 8.3)
- The player's choice is which filtered version to seal with, because Khoảng Lặng is built from the sealed version, not the raw one. (V3 section 8.2)

## 4. Entity rules

- Founding hypothesis: repeated and believed speech can live independently of the speaker; not only the dead but repeated words of the living also become seeds. (V3 section 6)
- Three player rules: P1 a record does not equal truth; P2 testimony shapes entities; P3 answering the speaker is testifying. (V3 section 6)
- Five derived rules: D1 the past cannot be changed; D2 phenomena are tied to the old audio network; D3 some entities develop awareness; D4 the living's words also spawn entities; D5 the Anchor rule. (V3 section 6)
- The Anchor rule has three independent axes: consciousness, identity, and the survival anchor. (V3 section 6)
- Resonance zones are the geological cavity along the wiring: basement B, the wire across the square, and the central station. Cutting one line cuts a feeding branch; cutting the central station cuts the main supply. (V3 section 6)
- Per-line entity states: Tràn (overflow) escapes into the remaining wire network and into houses; Giữ (held) is anchored to its real Khoảng Lặng and does not change; Yên (settled) no longer repeats or overflows, unconscious entities dissolve, conscious entities keep their identity but still need an anchor and leave when it is gone. (V3 section 6)
- "Yên" answers whether it still causes harm, not how far it can exist once the network is cut. (V3 section 6)
- The player reads line states on anh Tư điện's wiring diagram and knows which line is cut on which night, but not the hour; this is the only counter and it lives in the world. (V3 section 6)
- Entities follow the old audio network, so they do not appear at will. (V3 section 6, D2)
- The speaker has three roles at once: a containment tool, a concealment tool, and an overwrite tool used by Người Phát Ngôn. (V3 section 6)
- The folk rule "do not answer" is the raw form of P3. In Ending C that forbidden act becomes the way out: the whole town speaks. (V3 section 6)
- Eight main entities, each with vết thương / luật / dấu hiệu / at least two ways to resolve, and each with a survival anchor. (V3 section 9)
- Người Phát Ngôn has no password, cannot be negotiated or pitied, and cannot be resolved by understanding; it weakens from three of the four evidence links and dissolves with all four at the central station. (V3 section 9)
- Người Gõ Cửa chooses his own fate; staying on requires an anchor, so it is only possible in Endings B and C. (V3 section 9)
- Người Chờ Điểm Danh is unconscious repetition; Người Mang Tiếng Nước has no individual consciousness; Người Đứng Ngoài is anchored to Hùng, not to any line. (V3 section 9, V3 section 2)
- Nhi is an independent consciousness and can still be lost when the resonance zone is destroyed. (V3 section 9, V3 section 12)
- Chị Tuyết and bé Bống do not react to sound, only to names; if nobody calls their names they fade in silence when the last anchor is cut. (V3 section 9)
- Nhi's identity: subtitles call her Vọng after she chooses a name in Hồi 5; with three or more independent sources the identity is solid and she keeps her ground ("Con không phải Nhi."). (V3 section 8.4)
- Independent-witness candidates for Nhi: Hà, Lệ, bà Tám, bé Cốm, anh Tư; Khải and bà Mai do not count. (V3 section 8.4)
- Sự chú ý has four tiers: Yên, Nghe thấy, Nhận ra, Tìm đến. (V3 section 8.5)
- If touched at Tìm đến, Khải is pulled into a false Khoảng Lặng, does not die, and escapes elsewhere losing exactly one of three things: an unsealed recording; trust or a promise with the nearest NPC; or an entity in Giữ in that area reverting to Tràn. (V3 section 8.5)
- The first two lines (Đông and Chợ) are designed to be cut when the player lacks the tools, as a warning about consequences; from the Trường học line onward every line has at least one way to bring an entity to Giữ before it is cut. (V3 section 6)
- Line-cut schedule: Đông after night 4; Chợ and Trạm y tế after night 6; none on night 7 because the demolition team delayed due to rain; Trường học after night 9; Nhà máy và Đập after night 11; Trạm trung tâm cut at the ceremony on night 12. (V3 section 2, V3 section 10)
- The central-station line is the only line that can be prevented. (V3 section 2, V3 section 12)

## 5. Ending structure

- Three endings share three paths for the survival anchor of the remaining entities: A destroys it, B keeps the remaining network, C builds a new anchor out of community testimony. (V3 section 12)
- Combined with the two branches about the 2002 truth (Công khai / Giữ kín), that gives six final states. No ending is "best" or "official"; each has its own reward and cost. (V3 section 12)
- Making the truth public is a separate decision not automatically tied to any ending. (V3 section 12)
- Plan of the ceremony at 22:00 on night 12 at the Memorial Committee room in the square: choose ending direction, arrange witnesses and equipment, order the evidence links. (V3 section 12)
- The 43 seconds only execute: three beats of about 15 s, 15 s, 13 s, with no point demanding evidence or reconsidering the direction. (V3 section 12)
- Unlock conditions: A is always open, only safe when all main entities are Giữ or Yên; B requires preventing the central-station cut; C requires three independent witnesses who speak up for Nhi, the two names called, the Người Phát Ngôn weakened by three evidence links, enough residents speaking, and anh Tư's separate wiring finished in Hồi 7. (V3 section 12)
- Ending A: Khải and Hà cut the central-station line in a safe order; unconscious entities dissolve; conscious entities lose their survival anchor, including Người Phát Ngôn and Nhi (or Vọng). Nhi is not lost because she is unreal or unrecognised, but because the condition for her presence disappears. (V3 section 12)
- Ending B: the town accepts a shrunken network of the central station and the main wiring to basement B and the square; the four lines already cut stay silent. Reward: Nhi stays, relationships are kept, Người Gõ Cửa can stay as basement B's doorkeeper. Cost: people around the station and basement B keep being overwritten, Tràn entities still wander the cut part, and someone must run the nightly broadcast. (V3 section 12)
- Ending C: at exactly 02:16:34 the whole square answers "Còn ở đây." A small resonance zone of about 300 m around basement B and the square, linked by a short separate cable by anh Tư in Hồi 7, is fed by a testimony cycle: every night at 02:16 the residents answer. (V3 section 12)
- Ending C costs: Vọng cannot leave the anchor zone; if Khải leaves Khe Lạc she does not go with him. The cycle must be kept forever or the anchor and everything in it weakens. Entities without enough awareness or without a witness are not kept. (V3 section 12)
- Epilogue has two layers: night 13, six short scenes, with bà Tám's number of rice bowls changing by ending, then a short moment at 02:17; and one year later, a long-term card per character. (V3 section 12)

## 6. Chapter structure

- Seven chapters, compressed from about 33 hours to about 20 hours. (V3 section 10)
- Hồi 1 Người trở về (nights 1-2), Hồi 2 Những người không ngủ (nights 3-4), Hồi 3 Những lời khai (nights 5-6), Hồi 4 Cửa xả B (night 7), Hồi 5 Đứa trẻ không lớn (nights 8-9), Hồi 6 Người đã biết (nights 10-11), Hồi 7 02:17 (night 12). (V3 section 10)
- The player's question changes across the game: at the start "Chuyện gì đang xảy ra?", in the middle "Ai đang nói dối?", at the end "Nếu biết sự thật, mình có quyền quyết định sự tồn tại của người khác không?". (V3 section 10)
