# Source atlas — researched starting points, NOT a substitute for per-scene verification

**Inspected:** 2026-10-01. All links below are sources or search leads, not bundled media licenses. Reopen the original and check date, locality, relevance, updates, and rights before relying on a claim or using an image. A source about one province does not establish conditions throughout Vietnam.

## 2002 baseline: people, schools, infrastructure

1. **National Statistics Office of Vietnam, 2002 economic–social annual report** (historic period 2001–2003; hosted retrospectively): https://www.nso.gov.vn/du-lieu-va-so-lieu-thong-ke/2019/05/tinh-hinh-kinh-te-xa-hoi-nam-2002/ . Supports the fact of *variation* in school facilities, school investment and continued temporary classrooms. National figures cannot determine School No. 3's construction.
2. **Prime Minister, Decision 159/2002/QĐ-TTg, dated 15 November 2002:** https://chinhphu.vn/default.aspx?docid=11234&pageid=27160 . Approves school/classroom hardening program. **Chronology trap:** this date falls *after* the fictional September 2002 disaster. Do not treat the decision as evidence School No. 3 was rebuilt before the flood; check local implementation records for later upgrades.
3. **World Bank Microdata Library, Vietnam Household Living Standards Survey 2002 (VHLSS):** https://microdata.worldbank.org/index.php/catalog/2306 . Survey covers household possessions, housing, power, water, sanitation, education, and commune infrastructure. For details locate the actual relevant questionnaire/table and the selected region; the landing page itself provides no Khe Lạc-specific household inventory. Survey instruments include questions on commune roads/transport, telephone, markets, electricity and schools: https://microdata.worldbank.org/index.php/catalog/2306/datafile/F34 .
4. **Historical 2002 school-building records, Ninh Bình Provincial Historical Archives:** https://luutru.ninhbinh.gov.vn/tllt/mlvb/cong-trinh-xay-dung-nha-lop-hoc-truong-thcs-xa-kim-hai-huyen-kim-son-nam-2002-103361.html . Shows what a period-specific design-approval archival record can look like. **This is a secondary-school project in Ninh Bình, not a model plan for School No. 3.** Use as a lead toward similarly dated local school plans.
5. **Vietnamese rural school programs with individually dated/location-tagged projects, Vietnam Health/Education Volunteers:** https://www.vnah-hev.org/projects/schools--for-rural--ethnic-minority-children-in-vietnam/ . Contains multiple regional examples, including 2002 projects in Quảng Trị and An Giang. Verify original image captions/rights; do not merge different regional building types into one fictional town.
6. **ABC World Today report dated 14 August 2002 on Mekong flood-safe primary schools:** https://www.abc.net.au/worldtoday/archive/articles/2002/08/14/s648584.htm . Period-specific evidence of schools serving evacuation/refuge functions in the *Mekong Delta*. Analogy for multi-purpose school shelter design, **not** proof the remote reservoir valley's school followed the same scheme.

## Communal loudspeakers, media and technology changes

7. **Government explainer of Decree 49/2024/NĐ-CP (effective 1 July 2024):** https://xaydungchinhsach.chinhphu.vn/quy-dinh-moi-ve-hoat-dong-thong-tin-co-so-tu-1-7-dai-truyen-thanh-cap-xa-119240520163011356.htm . Describes administration and function of commune-level public broadcasting in the modern period. **Do not retroactively attribute this rule to 2002.**
8. **Government Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism, account of modern upgrades (2025):** https://bvhttdl.gov.vn/Pages/chi-tiet.aspx?url=%2Fnang-cap-he-thong-truyen-thanh-co-so-cau-noi-thong-tin-den-nguoi-dan-20251103121244987.htm . Describes an actual locality moving from FM to ICT-enabled broadcast facilities. Use to establish *transition and coexistence*, not a nationwide one-date replacement or specific Khe Lạc apparatus.
9. **Ministry of Science and Technology portal, commune broadcaster statistics 2018–2024:** https://mst.gov.vn/so-lieu-dai-truyen-thanh-cap-xa-co-so-truyen-thanh-truyen-hinh-cap-huyen-tu-nam-2018-2024-197240506144840522.htm . Points to a dated national series. Inspect downloadable underlying dataset before using quantitative claims.

## Settlement, architecture, culture and geographical differentiation

10. **National Institute of Architecture, Tràng An settlements and village landscape (2024):** https://vienkientrucquocgia.gov.vn/ket-noi-khong-gian-van-hoa-xa-hoi-vung-loi-va-vung-dem-danh-thang-trang-an-huong-toi-do-thi-di-san-vi-con-nguoi/ . Context for Ninh Bình/Tràng An settlement, roads, village spaces and architecture; **only a potential regional comparator**, not proof of a generic nationwide architectural form.
11. **UNESCO World Heritage Centre, Tràng An Landscape Complex:** https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1438 . Geographic/geological context for karst valley, villages and water in a particular northern location. Do not transpose its geology to an invented hydropower valley without investigating geology and dam feasibility.
12. **UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage, Vietnam listing:** https://ich.unesco.org/en/state/viet-nam-VN?info=elements-on-the-lists . Starting index for region- or community-specific practices; each practice must be localized before use. E.g. Then rituals belong to particular Tày/Nùng/Thái communities, not to every Vietnamese village: https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/practices-of-then-by-tay-nung-and-thai-ethnic-groups-in-viet-nam-01379 .

## Hydraulic plausibility — analogues, NOT engineering validation

13. **ANU 2002 historical research on Red River delta water infrastructure:** https://openresearch-repository.anu.edu.au/items/0b62db36-cd42-4521-a766-35c715fed0e7 . Broad historic context of water control in a specific delta; it does **not** validate V3's fictional mountainous reservoir flood geometry.
14. **Peer-reviewed 2005 research on Dầu Tiếng reservoir flood-checking:** https://std.vnuhcmjournal.com.vn/index.php/stdj/article/view/3095 . Demonstrates that hydraulic outcomes depend on actual reservoir, spillway and flood assumptions. Dầu Tiếng specifics must **not** be applied as Khe Lạc dimensions, flows or rescue times. Obtain site-relevant hydrological/topographic references and appropriately qualified review for pivotal technical plausibility.

## Primary image and provenance acquisition

- Search provincial historical archives, dated local newspaper photographs, town museum collections and institutional archives for a **specific region + year + object**. Cross-check captions and the photographic date; page publication in 2026 does not date the historical object 2026.
- Seek archival plans/photos for: pre-2002 school, 2002 emergency-assembly usage, 2003 repairs/memorial, current road/market/clinic, original/retrofitted broadcasting equipment, local river cross-sections and public construction.
- Record photographer/source owner, date/location, image URL, any cropping, permitted reuse, and credit requirements. Permission to view is not permission to place image, logo, real person's likeness, voice or trademark into the game.
- Avoid hotlinked or automatically downloaded web images in the commercial asset library until rights are resolved. Prefer authored textures and original assets informed by the *characteristics* of references.
