## Geographic anchor remains OPEN

V3 specifies terrain and narrative infrastructure; it does **not** select a real province. A sourced **proposal** for Đà Bắc / hồ Hòa Bình / sông Đà now exists at `docs/research/KHE_LAC_REGIONAL_REFERENCE.md` (status PROPOSED, owner has not locked it). Treat it as the leading hypothesis to research against, not as canon.

Candidate analogues might include northern/upper-river upland valleys, North-Central uplands, or other reservoir valleys; these are research **hypotheses only**, not equally suitable by default. Compare environmental coherence instead of stitching together northern delta, south-delta, upland and central-highlands features.

Before locking: investigate elevation/hydrology and runoff; rainy season in the **chosen** region in September; road/river circulation; housing and school construction; agricultural and market rhythms; local climate and plant communities; linguistic usage and respectful representation; realistic PA network morphology; and how all of these changed between 2002 and 2026.
