# Applying the skill to School No. 3 (example, not an asset implementation order)

## The question

How could a fictional Vietnamese school in a remote reservoir valley have been a credible 2002 evacuation/roll-call assembly point and still carry a legible trace of that event in 2026?

**CANON:** V3 §§4–5 establishes the remote valley, 2002 disaster, School No. 3 as assembly point, cô Vân's roll call, desks arranged as in 2002 and a haunted relationship between voice, omitted people and physical space. The script `ArtSource/Blender/SchoolFurniture/kl_school_furniture.py` already authors one desk and bench style; it is *not* historical evidence of the actual locality's school furnishing standard.

**Regional anchor:** not established by V3. Before proposing a final facade, research plausible upland reservoir localities and select an internal design hypothesis. Contrasting dated pictures of An Giang flood-safe schools, Quảng Trị schools and upper-northern highland temporary buildings is a way to *see regional variation*, **not** permission to collage them.

## A responsible 2002 / 2026 comparison

- **Before September 2002**: Was this particular school concrete, brick/tile, timber, temporary, or a mixed campus? A 2002 national report identifies variation; it does not identify School No. 3. Search local dated school construction registers, school photos and household infrastructure records. Do not assume every rural school was bamboo, or already met a modern design standard.
- **After the disaster**: What survived the water level? Which walls, flooring, furniture, doors, storage cupboards, loose papers, notice boards, wiring and equipment could be saved versus replaced? Flood marks require a plausible height, sediment route and maintenance history. Decide from topography and level plan, not a universal dirt overlay.
- **2003–2025**: Who repaired the school and for what continuing community use? V3 deliberately retains the desks' arrangement; retaining placement does not automatically freeze all infrastructure, furniture or notices for 24 years.
- **2026**: Everyday survival may include patched plaster, revised electrical fixtures, newer security hardware, locally repaired timber and an old board whose symbolic importance is less obvious than the player's later realization. Exactly which traces the player first notices depends on chapter knowledge.

## Useful historically sourced leads

- NSO 2002 annual statistics: school infrastructure varied; historic national context only (SOURCE_ATLAS §2002 item 1).
- School-hardening Decision 159 was dated **15 November 2002**, AFTER the fictional September flood. It may inform later changes, never an assumed earlier upgrade (item 2).
- A 2002 ABC report documents flood-safe primary schools doubling as refuges in the **Mekong Delta**. Shelter function is an interesting *analogy*, but this does not identify the upland school design (item 6).
- A Ninh Bình archival item references a design-approval document in 2002 for a **secondary** school; an example of the archival evidence type to seek, not a prefab plan for this school (item 4).

## Promising visual hypotheses — DESIGN PROPOSALS, not historical conclusions

- The scene may reveal structural change where old and new materials physically meet: a replastered lower wall, reused door timber or patched conduit following an actual repair history.
- A mundane layer should be present before supernatural artifacts: ordinary class notices, carefully reused furniture, cleaning marks, storage habits and safe movement through halls.
- The old loudspeaker wire may cross the route to the school if supported by the project's own PA line diagram; the wire's physical route must be coordinated with the story's cut-line network, not invented solely for visual appeal.
- What the player can learn from the classroom's physical register should remain distinct from what the **hầm B** official register establishes. A familiar-looking clue may be an echo, but not an unauthorized early reveal of the omitted victims' true identities.

## Asset and experience handoff

1. Existing art: confirm current primary G1 map and actual desk/bench import; do not rebuild every asset from scratch.
2. Research output: 2–3 dated/located reference examples for exterior shell, interior school fittings and 2002/2026 repair history. Capture source and use rights, not just thumbnails.
3. Design output: one grounded plan of circulation from assembly space to route out of the school; furniture assembly dimensions clearly marked as **modelled/provisional**; clue sightlines and collision verified in the real game.
4. Player test: visually distinguish current, echo and false geometry, without treating all unnatural-looking geometry as bad modeling. Judge rendered shots and the player's understanding, not mesh counts.
5. Open questions: region, original 2002 school construction, what repairs occurred, whether existing sources are sufficient to depict the chosen school's cultural specificity, and when the player can recognize the two absent identities.

The desired improvement is *credible normal life with a precisely wrong memory*, not simply a more dilapidated classroom.
